## A party, your way

Promenade is a mobile game featuring party-style mini games. Play solo, join a public multiplayer session, or play in a private multiplayer session.

## Current platform

Promenade is currently iOS only.

## Common questions

### Can I play alone or with other people?

Promenade's party-style mini games are designed for solo play and for private or public multiplayer sessions.

### Which mobile platform is it for?

The current platform is iOS.

### Is Promenade available to download?

Promenade is in development and has not been released. Official download links and pricing will be added when confirmed.
