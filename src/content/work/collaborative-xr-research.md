## The engineering context

This academic research prototype explored a shared immersive environment for support-group interactions. The technical work covered multi-user architecture, synchronized avatars, shared media, and spatial interfaces.

## Contribution

Research software design and implementation used Unity, Meta XR, Netcode for GameObjects, and HTTP-based communication.

## Outcome and limits

Prototype architecture and interaction systems were developed. This overview makes no claim about clinical effectiveness, a commercial release, or a Prather Software client relationship. Additional implementation details and supporting artifacts will be published only after review and permission where required.
