## The research context

This graduate thesis explored adaptive interventions within an immersive classroom environment. The technical work involved Unreal Engine, virtual reality, and eye tracking.

## Contribution

The project involved research and development of the immersive learning environment and evaluation across three study conditions.

## Outcome and limits

The findings were not statistically significant for the primary quiz outcome. This project is presented as academic research and engineering experience; it is not evidence of improved learning outcomes or a Prather Software commercial engagement. Additional study details and supporting artifacts remain subject to publication review and any required permission.
