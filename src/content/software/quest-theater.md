## Your content, theater-style

Quest Theater is a utility app for Meta Quest devices that enables theater-style viewing of content cast from an iPhone over AirPlay.

## Casting support

iPhone casting uses AirPlay. Android casting support is planned; it is separate from the current iPhone casting capability.

## Common questions

### What does Quest Theater do?

Quest Theater brings content cast from an iPhone into theater-style viewing on a Meta Quest device.

### Does it support Android casting?

Android casting is planned. The current casting capability described here uses AirPlay from an iPhone.

### Can I download it now?

Quest Theater is coming soon and has not been released. Its official listing and pricing will be added when confirmed.
