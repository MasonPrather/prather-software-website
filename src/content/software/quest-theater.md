## Your content, theater-style

Quest Theater is a utility app for Meta Quest devices that enables theater-style viewing of content cast from an iPhone over AirPlay.

## Casting support

iPhone casting uses AirPlay. Android casting support is planned; it is separate from the current iPhone casting capability.
